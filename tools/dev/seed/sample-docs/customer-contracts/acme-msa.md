# ACME - master services agreement

**Fictional. Sample data for local development.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Counterparty | ACME Industries |
| Effective | 2026-01-15 |
| Term | 24 months, auto-renewing for 12 unless either side gives 60 days notice |
| Governing law | England and Wales |

## 1. Services

The supplier provides the software described in each statement of work, together with support
during the counterparty's business hours.

## 2. Data

Each side remains the controller of the data it supplies. The supplier processes it only to
provide the services, and deletes or returns it within 30 days of termination on request.

## 3. Liability

Each side's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months, except for
death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.
